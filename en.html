Time Series · FX Risk

COP/USD Exchange Rate Forecast with Risk Bands

Banco de la República — 416 months (1991–2026)

Business Impact

Dashboard that turns an ARIMA + GARCH model into the probability that the dollar exceeds a reference rate in each month, useful for budgeting. It showed that the point forecast does not beat a random walk, but the GARCH bands are well calibrated: 6.7% of months fall outside the 95% band, versus 11.7% with constant variance.
